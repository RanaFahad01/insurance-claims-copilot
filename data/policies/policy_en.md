# Norvale Mutual: Home and Vehicle Plan (HG-Standard)

Fictional policy wording, invented for a software demo. Norvale Mutual does not exist and this is not real insurance cover. Version 1.0.

## C1.1 | Definitions
"You" means the policyholder named in the policy schedule. "We" means Norvale Mutual. "Insured home" means the home at the address on the schedule, including its fixed garage and fixed outbuildings such as a shed or a storage room that is part of the building. "Contents" means your movable belongings kept in the insured home, including bicycles. "Insured vehicle" means a private car or motorcycle registered to you and listed on the schedule. "Sudden" means happening within a short, identifiable time and not expected. "Gradual" means developing over days, weeks or months. "Unoccupied" means that no person has stayed overnight in the insured home.

## C1.2 | What is insured
We insure the insured home, its contents and your insured vehicles against the events described in sections 2 to 6 of this policy. Cover is subject to the exclusions in this policy, the limits in C7.1 and the conditions in C7.2 and C7.3.

## C1.3 | Where the policy applies
Cover applies to the insured home and to events that happen in the member states of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. For personal belongings away from home, see C4.3. Property that is outside these countries, and events that happen outside them, are not covered.

## C1.4 | When the policy applies
Cover applies to events that happen during the policy period shown on the schedule. If the policy is renewed without a gap, the new period continues the cover. Events that happened before the period started, or after it ended, are not covered.

## C2.1 | Water damage: covered
We cover damage to the insured home and its contents caused by the sudden and unforeseen escape of water from fixed water pipes, tanks and heating systems, and from washing machines, dishwashers and similar fixed appliances, including their hoses. We also cover the reasonable cost of finding the source of the leak and opening up walls or floors to repair it. Water that escapes gradually is excluded by C2.3, flooding is excluded by C2.4, and damage from poor maintenance is excluded by C8.2. Frost damage is dealt with in C2.2.

## C2.2 | Frost damage to pipes: covered, with exceptions
We cover damage caused by water pipes, tanks or heating systems freezing and bursting. We do not cover it if the insured home was unoccupied for more than 30 days in a row immediately before the damage. This exception does not apply if the water system had been drained and the heating switched off before the home was left, or if a person checked the home at least once every 7 days during that time.

## C2.3 | Water damage: gradual leaks excluded
We do not cover damage caused by water that escaped gradually or repeatedly over a period of time, such as a slow leak, seepage, condensation, rising damp or a constantly dripping fitting. We cover water only when it escapes suddenly (see C2.1).

## C2.4 | Water damage: flood and groundwater excluded
We do not cover damage caused by flooding from rivers, lakes or the sea, by rising groundwater, or by sewage or drain water flowing back in from outside the insured home. Rain or snow that enters after storm damage is dealt with in C5.2.

## C3.1 | Fire, explosion and lightning: covered
We cover damage to the insured home, its contents and insured vehicles caused by fire, smoke from a fire, explosion and lightning. This includes fires caused by an electrical fault or by an appliance. Exclusions are in C3.2 and C8.1.

## C3.2 | Fire: deliberate acts and gross negligence excluded
We do not cover fire damage that you, or a person living with you, caused on purpose. We also do not cover fire damage caused by gross negligence, meaning a conscious disregard of an obvious and serious risk, such as leaving an open flame unattended for a long time after being warned of the danger.

## C3.3 | Firefighting and extinguishing damage: covered
We cover damage caused while putting out a fire or stopping it from spreading, including damage from water or foam and damage caused by the fire brigade breaking in or taking down parts of the building. This is treated as part of the fire under C3.1. It is not treated as water damage under section 2.

## C4.1 | Theft and burglary at home: covered
We cover theft of contents from the insured home, including attempted theft, and the cost of repairing damage done by the thieves. Items kept in shared areas of a building, such as common storage rooms or shared hallways, are covered only if they were kept in a locked storage unit assigned to you. Exclusions are in C4.4.

## C4.2 | Theft from vehicles and outbuildings: covered
We cover theft of an insured vehicle, theft of items from an insured vehicle, and theft of items from a fixed outbuilding that is part of the insured home, such as a garage or shed. Exclusions are in C4.4.

## C4.3 | Personal belongings away from home: covered
We cover the theft of your personal belongings anywhere in the area described in C1.3, while you are carrying them or while they are inside a locked room or a locked vehicle. The most we pay for this cover is stated in C7.1. Exclusions are in C4.4.

## C4.4 | Theft: unsecured and unattended property excluded
We do not cover theft of items that were left unattended in a public place, or left in an unlocked home, outbuilding or vehicle, or left in view inside an unattended vehicle. We do not cover theft by a person who lives with you or who was given a key by you.

## C5.1 | Storm, hail and falling objects: covered
We cover damage to the insured home, its contents and insured vehicles caused by storm, meaning gale-force wind or stronger, by hail, and by the weight of snow. We also cover damage caused by objects that a storm blows or knocks onto them, such as roof tiles or branches. A roof, window or skylight broken by a storm or by hail is covered.

## C5.2 | Rain and snow entering after storm damage: covered
We cover damage to the interior and contents of the insured home caused by rain, snow or melted snow that enters through an opening that a storm event covered by C5.1 has created, such as a broken window or missing roof tiles. This applies even though the damage is caused by water. Flooding is not covered (see C2.4).

## C5.3 | Storm: open or defective openings excluded
We do not cover damage caused by rain, snow or hail that enters through windows, doors or skylights that were left open, or through a roof, window or door that was already damaged or badly maintained before the storm. See also C8.2.

## C6.1 | Vehicle collision damage: covered
We cover damage to an insured vehicle caused by a collision with another vehicle, a person or an object, including when the insured vehicle is parked. If the other driver is not known, the collision must be reported to the police within 48 hours (see C7.3). Exclusions are in C6.3.

## C6.2 | Vehicle impact on the home: covered
We cover damage to the walls, doors, roof and windows of the insured home, and of its fixed garage and outbuildings, caused by the impact of a vehicle, including a vehicle driven by someone else.

## C6.3 | Vehicles: commercial use and unlicensed drivers excluded
We do not cover damage to a vehicle while it is used for hire or reward, for example as a taxi, while it is driven by a person who does not hold a valid licence, or while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

## C7.1 | Sums insured and limits
The most we pay for one event is EUR 300,000 for the insured home, EUR 50,000 for contents, and the market value for an insured vehicle. Within contents, the most we pay is EUR 2,500 for any single item, EUR 5,000 in total for jewellery and watches, and EUR 3,000 in total for personal belongings away from home under C4.3.

## C7.2 | Deductible
You pay the first EUR 250 of each claim for the insured home and its contents, and the first EUR 500 of each claim for an insured vehicle. We take the deductible off the amount we pay.

## C7.3 | Notifying a claim
You must tell us about damage within 30 days of finding it. Theft, burglary and collisions with an unknown driver must also be reported to the police within 48 hours, and you must give us the police report number. If you tell us late and this makes the claim harder for us to assess, we may reduce or refuse the payment.

## C8.1 | General exclusions
We do not cover damage caused by war, terrorism or nuclear events, by earthquake, landslide or other ground movement, or by anything you did on purpose.

## C8.2 | Wear, tear and lack of maintenance: excluded
We do not cover damage caused by wear and tear, corrosion or rot, or by a failure to keep the property in good condition, such as an old roof or worn pipes that you knew needed repair.

## C8.3 | False or misleading claims
If you give false or misleading information in a claim, or you exaggerate your loss, we may refuse the whole claim.

## C9.1 | How claims are assessed
A claims adjuster reviews every claim and makes the final decision. Software may be used to prepare a summary of the claim and the relevant policy clauses, but no claim is approved or refused automatically.

## C9.2 | Language of claims
You may write to us in English or Spanish. We reply in the language you used.
